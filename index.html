WHO WE ARE

A COMMUNITY-DRIVEN STRIKING CLUB IN BANGSAR 🥊 DEDICATED TO DISCIPLINE, AUTHENTIC THAI PAD ROUNDS, AND EMPOWERING EVERY ATHLETE.

We founded The Backyard to tear down the sterile, overcrowded commercial fitness gym. No fake cardio kickboxing, no 30-person rooms where the coach never learns your name. Just authentic 10-station circuits, dedicated coach pad work, and maximum 12 people on the mats so you make real technical progress every single round.

THE FORMAT

THE 10-STATION GAUNTLET

Every student hits pads with the coach. Every round is timed. Nobody stands idle.

01–05

Heavy Bag Combat Alley

Dutch kickboxing 4-count combos, teep precision push kicks, liver shovel hooks, and knee clinch mechanics on heavy 6ft leather bags across 5 stations.

06 CORE ROUND

1-on-1 Coach Pad Round

Real Thai pads. Head Kru Adli or Fahmi calibrates your timing, slips counters, corrects your balance, and pushes your output under live pad pressure.

07

Assault Bike Engine

High-wattage air resistance intervals simulating championship rounds. Develop the lung capacity to throw 100% power in round 3 when your opponent is wilting.

08–10

Striking Strength & Core

Kettlebell rotational snatches, medicine ball slam downs, isometric core rotational bracing, and explosive plyometric push mechanics tailored for knockouts.
